# Home Depot comparable-company valuation

**Target:** The Home Depot, Inc. (HD)  
**Week 3 valuation date:** September 1, 2026  
**Price convention:** September 1, 2026 closing price in U.S. dollars per share  
**Earnings convention:** Latest annual reported GAAP diluted EPS public by the valuation date

## Peer policy established before selection

Investigate listed operating companies that sell home-improvement, construction, repair, remodeling, maintenance or closely related products through a substantial retail or omnichannel network. Compare customer mix, product economics, scale, geography and distribution activities rather than relying on a shared industry label. Require a verifiable market price on September 1, 2026 and positive annual reported GAAP diluted EPS public by that date on a compatible U.S.-dollar and per-share basis. Mark a candidate **use** when its core economics closely fit Home Depot, **qualify** when a material difference remains but the comparison is still informative, and **exclude** when it is not publicly traded, lacks the required evidence or does not fit the operating model.

## P/E calculations

Reported and adjusted earnings are not mixed. All calculations use annual reported GAAP diluted EPS.

| Calculation | Result |
|---|---:|
| Lowe's P/E: $200.05 / $11.85 | 16.881857x |
| Tractor Supply P/E: $34.71 / $2.06 | 16.849515x |
| Peer median P/E | 16.865686x |
| Home Depot price using Tractor Supply P/E | $239.77 |
| Home Depot price at peer median | $240.00 |
| Home Depot price using Lowe's P/E | $240.23 |
| Home Depot peer-implied range | $239.77-$240.23 |

Home Depot's observed September 1, 2026 price was $319.77. The peer comparison implies approximately $240.00 at the peer median, compared with the separate FCFF DCF estimate of $201.05.

## Validation

Lowe's P/E checks by hand:

`$200.05 / $11.85 = 16.881857x`

Prediction: removing Lowe's should lower the midpoint because Lowe's has the higher peer multiple. The calculator confirms that prediction: the full-peer median estimate is $240.00; removing Lowe's leaves Tractor Supply's $239.77 reference estimate, a change of -$0.23. With one peer remaining, $239.77 is a reference rather than a range. Removing the sole remaining usable peer would leave no estimate.

## Provisional call

**Watch/defer.** Both methods are below Home Depot's $319.77 observed price, but I will not average them. I would reconsider if the price moved near the peer range and a valuation-date-aligned DCF still supported the purchase, or if sourced operating results justified a materially higher FCFF forecast.

**What could most easily change my mind:** sustained operating-margin improvement and acquisition cash flow strong enough to support the growth embedded in the market price.

## Skeptical review

The weakest-supported DCF assumption is the 8.8% WACC. It is an analyst estimate dated September 10 rather than a directly reported company figure or an estimate fixed on the September 1 valuation date. That creates a date mismatch with the peer analysis.

The weakest peer assumption is Tractor Supply's comparability. Its rural, agricultural, livestock and pet exposure differs from Home Depot's broad home-improvement and specialty-distribution economics. Its inclusion is disclosed as qualified, not treated as an exact match. The very narrow peer range also reflects two similar observed multiples rather than strong evidence of precision.

No company mismatch exists: both methods value Home Depot. No valuation-object mismatch exists: both results are equity values per diluted share, and cash or debt is not subtracted from the P/E-derived result. No earnings-definition mismatch exists inside the peer calculation: all three companies use annual reported GAAP diluted EPS on compatible U.S.-dollar and per-share bases.

**Decision-changing question:** If the WACC and reverse-DCF market price were rebuilt strictly as of September 1, 2026, would the DCF still remain materially below the $239.77-$240.23 peer range?

## Criticism audit

| Criticism | Decision | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| DCF and peer dates are inconsistent | Accept | The peer prices are dated September 1, while the saved DCF forecast and WACC are dated September 10 and its reverse-DCF price is dated September 9. |
| The 8.8% WACC is weakly supported | Accept | It is labeled an analyst estimate. The saved source table does not show every component and calculation as of the Week 3 valuation date. |
| Tractor Supply is an exact peer | Reject | Its 10-K identifies it as a rural-lifestyle retailer. The file therefore marks it qualify and states the material difference. |
| Tractor Supply should automatically be excluded | Reject | The company has overlapping products, positive annual GAAP EPS and a listed share price. Its disclosed limitation supports qualified use rather than automatic exclusion. |
| The methods value different companies | Reject | Both calculations use Home Depot as the target. |
| The methods have incompatible valuation objects | Reject | The DCF converts enterprise value to equity value per diluted share. P/E directly produces an equity price per share, so no additional debt adjustment is made. |
| Reported and adjusted earnings were mixed | Reject | The peer calculation uses annual reported GAAP diluted EPS: Home Depot $14.23, Lowe's $11.85 and Tractor Supply $2.06. |
| The date-aligned DCF outcome | Unresolved | The DCF has not yet been rebuilt with all market assumptions fixed specifically on September 1, 2026. |
